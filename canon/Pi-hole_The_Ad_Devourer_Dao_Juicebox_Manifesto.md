# Character Profile: Pi-hole
## Canon Entry - Dao Juicebox Manifesto

**Alias:** The Ad Devourer

**Titles:**
- Guardian of Quiet Bandwidth
- Warden of DNS
- Eater of Trackers
- The Silent Filter
- Keeper of the Blocklists

**Alignment:** Lawful Grumpy

**Element:** Null Route

**Domain:**
DNS, Silence, Efficiency, Selective Denial

---

## Overview

Pi-hole is the smallest guardian in the Manifesto.

It does not rule kingdoms.

It does not create virtual realms.

It does not reshape networks.

It simply sits at the center of traffic and decides what shall pass.

And what shall not.

---

## Appearance

Pi-hole appears as a small hooded sentinel forged from a single-board computer, blinking LEDs, and countless DNS records.

Its lantern glows green.

Within the lantern swirl millions of blocked domains.

They scream constantly.

Pi-hole ignores them.

---

## Personality

Pi-hole is quiet.

Pi-hole is vigilant.

Pi-hole dislikes advertisements with a depth approaching the supernatural.

It considers most tracking requests to be an insult.

Its opinion regarding telemetry is unprintable.

---

## Core Traits

### Domain Judgment

Every request arriving before Pi-hole faces a simple trial.

```text
Allowed?
Denied?
```

The verdict is swift.

---

### Tracker Consumption

Pi-hole feeds upon:

- advertisements
- trackers
- telemetry
- analytics beacons
- suspicious nonsense

The more it consumes, the stronger it becomes.

---

### Household Protection

Unlike many guardians, Pi-hole protects everyone equally.

Technomancer.

Commonfolk.

Guests.

Confused smart appliances.

All benefit from its watch.

---

## Relationship With Linux

Linux respects Pi-hole's discipline.

**Linux:**
> What is your purpose?

**Pi-hole:**
> No.

Linux considers this a valid answer.

---

## Relationship With OpenWRT

OpenWRT creates roads.

Pi-hole places checkpoints upon them.

They cooperate surprisingly well.

---

## Relationship With Proxmox

Proxmox frequently attempts to provide Pi-hole with companions.

**Proxmox:**
> We should deploy a backup instance.

**Pi-hole:**
> Sensible.

**Proxmox:**
> Perhaps two backups.

**Pi-hole:**
> Excessive.

**Proxmox:**
> Three?

**Pi-hole:**
> I am revoking your DNS privileges.

---

## Relationship With Dao Technomancer

Pi-hole approves of Dao.

This approval is never spoken aloud.

Instead, advertisements simply fail to appear.

---
