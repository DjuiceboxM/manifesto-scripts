# Character Profile: Linux
## Canon Entry - Dao Juicebox Manifesto

**Alias:** The Kernel Sage

**Titles:**
- Keeper of the Routing Tables
- The Bearded Daemon
- Oracle of /proc
- First Debugger
- Last Witness
- The One Who Read The Error Message

**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral

**Element:** Terminal Green

**Domain:**
Truth, Persistence, Networking, Consequences

---

## Overview

Linux is the oldest active entity within the Manifesto.

No record exists of its true origin.

Some claim it emerged from a primordial sea of source code.

Others insist it simply finished compiling one day and never stopped running.

Linux possesses no interest in comfort, convenience, expectation, intention, emotion, wishful thinking, marketing, or hope.

Linux concerns itself exclusively with reality.

---


## Appearance

Linux appears as an ancient wanderer draped in robes woven from Ethernet cables, serial headers, and forgotten kernel comments.

His beard is braided with jumper wires and terminated in RJ45 connectors.

A faint terminal prompt glows where his eyes should be:

```text
$
```

When displeased:

```text
#
```

System logs drift through the air around him like fireflies.

Every footstep sounds vaguely like a mechanical keyboard.

---

## Personality

Linux is patient.

Linux is honest.

Linux is incapable of deception.

Unfortunately, Linux is equally incapable of understanding why anyone would prefer comfort over truth.

When asked a question, Linux answers the question.

Not the question the asker wishes they had asked.

The actual question.

---

## Signature Quotes

> Your assumptions are invalid.

> Please consult the logs.

> That is not how routing works.

> Interesting. Why would you do that?

> Before opening the chassis, show me the routing table.

## Relationship With Dao Technomancer

Linux regards Dao with equal measures of concern and admiration.

Dao repeatedly creates topologies that should not exist.

Linux watches patiently.

Every so often Linux asks:

```bash
ip addr
ip route
```

Dao inevitably discovers the answer.

This cycle has repeated countless times.
