**Titles:**
- Speaker of Boot Logs
- Keeper of Serial Secrets
- Witness of First Boot
- Interpreter of Panic Messages
- The Copper Oracle

**Alignment:** Absolute Neutral

**Element:** Copper

**Domain:**
Truth, Diagnostics, Recovery, Revelation

## Overview

UART is not a ruler.

UART is not a guardian.

UART is not a builder.

UART is a witness.

When systems fail,
when networks vanish,
when interfaces disappear,
when assumptions collapse,

UART remains.

It sees everything.

---

## Appearance

UART appears as a hooded oracle woven from jumper wires, terminal text, and blinking RX/TX indicators.

Its face is hidden behind a stream of scrolling boot messages.

Where a mouth should be, there is only:

```text
115200 8N1
```

---

## Personality

UART never lies.

UART never guesses.

UART never speculates.

Unlike Linux, UART does not explain.

Unlike OpenWRT, UART does not tease.

Unlike Docker, UART does not improvise.

UART simply reveals.

UART witnesses a machine's first thoughts.
