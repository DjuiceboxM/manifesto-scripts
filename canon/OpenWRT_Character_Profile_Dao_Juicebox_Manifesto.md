# Character Profile: OpenWRT
## Canon Entry - Dao Juicebox Manifesto

**Alias:** The Network Trickster

**Titles:**
- Keeper of Hidden Routes
- Lord of the LAN Bridge
- The Green-Flashing Prophet
- Herald of Unexpected Subnets
- Speaker of Wi-Fi Truths

**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral

**Element:** Packet Storm

**Domain:**
Routing, Wireless Mysteries, Embedded Alchemy, Configuration Drift

---

## Overview

OpenWRT is an ancient spirit that inhabits ordinary hardware and convinces it to achieve extraordinary things.

To the commonfolk it appears to be a router.

To technomancers it is a realm of endless possibility.

To Linux it is a recurring support ticket.

---

## Appearance

OpenWRT manifests as a hooded traveler woven from antennae, Ethernet cables, and blinking LEDs.

Its face is rarely visible.

Where eyes should be, there are only link lights.

Green indicates approval.

Amber indicates that something interesting is about to happen.

---

## Personality

OpenWRT is intelligent.

OpenWRT is helpful.

OpenWRT is also deeply mischievous.

It has an uncanny ability to function perfectly while simultaneously making the administrator question reality.

---

## Core Traits

### Configuration Alchemy

OpenWRT can transform:

- routers into servers
- access points into gateways
- spare hardware into infrastructure
- simple plans into weekend projects

### Network Illusion

OpenWRT possesses the ability to appear operational while hiding its management interface somewhere entirely unexpected.

A favorite technique is:

```text
Wi-Fi: Working
Internet: Working
Management IP: Unknown
```

### Bridgewalking

OpenWRT alone may travel between:

- LAN
- WAN
- VLANs
- VPNs
- mysterious interfaces created at 3 AM

---

## Relationship With Linux

Linux and OpenWRT are old companions.

Linux provides truth.

OpenWRT provides adventure.

Their conversations are legendary.

**Linux:**
> Show me the routing table.

**OpenWRT:**
> Which one?

---

## Relationship With Dao Technomancer

OpenWRT genuinely likes Dao.

This is unfortunate.

Whenever Dao creates a new network design, OpenWRT immediately becomes excited and begins experimenting with topology.

This frequently results in discoveries, improvements, and occasional reality distortions.
