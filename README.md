# Canon Origin
 
## S00E01
### The GDF15 Glitch & The Infinite Loop of Puke
 
The earliest recognized episode of the Dao Juicebox Manifesto.
 
What began as an attempt to understand Hyperemesis Gravidarum evolved into the first fully realized interaction between Juicebox and Kenji.
 
In this episode:
 
- Medical terminology becomes systems analysis.
- Technical concepts become accessible metaphors.
- Engineering logic meets human compassion.
 
For the first time, the dual perspective that would define future Manifesto episodes emerged:
 
### Juicebox
 
Examines complex problems as interconnected systems.
 
### Kenji
 
Translates those systems into human experiences.
 
---
 
## Episode Abstract
 
Hyperemesis Gravidarum (HG) is a severe and potentially dangerous pregnancy condition involving persistent nausea, vomiting, dehydration, electrolyte imbalance, and nutritional deficits.
 
Rather than treating HG as an abstract medical condition, the episode explores:
 
- Biological feedback loops
- System failures
- Resource depletion
- Monitoring strategies
- Compassionate interventions
 
The discussion later evolves into:
 
### Telemetry of the Tummy
 
A HomeLab-inspired hydration-monitoring concept built around:
 
- ESP32
- Home Assistant
- Load Cells
- Automated hydration tracking
 
Demonstrating one of the Manifesto's core principles:
 
> Technology should reduce suffering, not merely demonstrate capability.
